S7 · The Shapers

Competing dungeon-masters. Not IN the world's causal ledger — they apply pressure on it, manifesting at intersections of region × culture × society.

Gods & Domainscompeting divine agents (War, Creation, Death, Chaos...); faith power from worship; mortals can ascend; intervene via events, blessings, disasters
Moral Cosmologymorality as a force field the shapers contest — not a stat on entities but a pressure that flows through cultures and regions, changing what time-acquisition is sanctioned
The Loremaster Functionworld-level dramaturgy: which latent conflicts get fueled, which regions get catastrophes, pacing of history — the DM seat, possibly LLM-assisted

S4 · Life

Conscious entities — and all living things on a continuum of mind. Conversation = the channel between private Minds: exploration of the shared goal-hypergraph, expression of intent; nothing telepathic, information only moves by exchange.

Time MetabolismTHE goal function: finite time, consumption, transfer, death — re-derived from first principles
Body Assemblyfeet→…→head, tens of thousands of part variants, blueprint-composed seamless entity + rig
Identityattributes, personality, name, race, life stage
Mindprivate semantic memory graph + embeddings; deterministic context-assembly → cheap fast LLM; conversations decomposed back into the graph
Agencygame-theoretic decisions: goals, plans, risk; every action an active, remembered choice
Relationship Hypergraphsocial/familial/sexual/employment/commercial/rivalry/communal n-ary edges
Faunaanimals = same Life stack with thinner Mind/Agency (instinct-weighted, no LLM); predation, domestication, ecology feed the time economy
