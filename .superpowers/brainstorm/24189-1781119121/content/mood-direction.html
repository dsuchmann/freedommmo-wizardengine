Which golden-hour mood direction feels right?

Same in-game steppe screenshot, four different grading/atmosphere directions (CSS approximations — the real shader version will be richer). This sets the personality of the whole lighting system.

A — Filmic teal & orange

Modern cinematic: warm amber light, shadows pushed toward teal. High contrast, controlled saturation. Think modern AAA "magic hour".

B — Painterly golden glow

Lush, warm, saturated — visible sun glow bleeding across the scene, soft bloom. Think Ghibli sunsets / Eastward. Optimistic rather than ominous.

C — Muted & melancholy

Desaturated, cool shadows, heavy vignette, restrained warm light. Quiet, lonely, atmospheric. Think Nordic indie / Sword & Sworcery.

D — High-drama chiaroscuro

Strong directional light vs deep shadow, vivid sky color crashing into darkness. Maximum drama: long black shadows, burning highlights. Think Blasphemous / Darkest Dungeon energy in daylight.

Click the one that matches the feeling you want at sunrise/sunset. You can also mix ("B's glow but C's restraint") — say so in the terminal.
