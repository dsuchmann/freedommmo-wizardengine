## Aesthetic
A sun-and-salt tropical-coast hamlet built from what the reef and surf provide: porous cream-coral reef blocks, smooth crushed-shell tabby render, salt-bleached driftwood planking, and breezy woven golden palm matting on bamboo, all dusted with golden sand and flecked with iridescent shell. The architectural identity is SUN-BLEACHED COASTAL STRUCTURE — every wall reads first as ONE solid load-bearing surface, its value coming from dark coursing joints, deep plank-seam shadow, pitted coral holes and a sandy stone footing, NEVER from a bright or stark-white body. The lightest faces (tabby, driftwood) hold at a MID dressed-marble / mid silver-grey value, never near-white. Palette: warm cream and coral-pink, mid silver-grey driftwood with faded teal flecks, golden-tan woven palm with green bamboo, dark driftwood-brown members, weathered teal-glass glints and iridescent shell sheen.
