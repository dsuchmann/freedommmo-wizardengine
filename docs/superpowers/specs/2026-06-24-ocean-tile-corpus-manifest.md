## Aesthetic
A working-sea fishing settlement of stilt-houses and pier shacks raised over open salt water, built entirely from tar, rope, net and sea-bleached wood. The architectural identity is WEATHERED MARITIME TIMBER — every wall reads first as one solid load-bearing working-boat-grade surface, its value coming from black pitch coatings, dark spray-stained grooves, deep shadow laps and a salt-crusted footing, never from a bright bleached body. Even the palest sea-bleached driftwood-grey is held at a MID cool tone with dark grain and shadow doing the structural work, never near-white. Palette: black-brown tar pitch, golden-brown stripped log, mid driftwood and sea-bleached silver-grey, faded marine teal, tan knotted net, cork-and-glass floats, mussel-shell and waterline stain.
