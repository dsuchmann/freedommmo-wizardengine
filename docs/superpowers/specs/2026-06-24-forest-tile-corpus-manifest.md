## Aesthetic
A deep temperate woodland village built entirely of timber: round golden-pine logs, coursed honey-oak planks, dark-oak frames packed with mid warm-grey plaster, and shaggy russet bark-slab huts, all damp with temperate moss. The architectural identity is WARM WOOD STRUCTURE — every wall reads first as one solid load-bearing timber surface, its value coming from dark joints, deep chinking shadow and grey stone footing, never from a bright body. Palette: golden honey + amber + russet woods, dark chestnut-oak members, mid grey-cream plaster, mossy grey-green stone and damp lichen flecks.
