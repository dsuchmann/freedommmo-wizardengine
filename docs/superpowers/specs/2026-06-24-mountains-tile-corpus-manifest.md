## Aesthetic
A high-fantasy highland settlement carved from the bones of the mountain itself: precisely cut grey granite ashlar, thin dry-stacked blue-grey slate, dark-oak timber frames packed with mortared fieldstone rubble, and monumental dwarven megastone banded with glowing bronze runes. The architectural identity is COLD CUT STONE STRUCTURE — every wall reads first as one solid, continuous, load-bearing masonry surface whose value comes from DARK mortar lines, recessed coursing shadow and a heavy rusticated footing, NEVER from a bright pale body. The single warm break is the oak-and-rubble wall and the bronze runic inlay; everything else holds in saturated cool greys, slate blues and steely shadow. Palette: speckled mid-grey granite with warm tan mortar veining, blue-grey laminated slate with steel shadows, dark chestnut-oak timber against cool grey rubble in pale lime mortar, and cold megastone lit by warm verdigris-and-bronze accents.
