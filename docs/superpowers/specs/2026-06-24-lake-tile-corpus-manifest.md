## Aesthetic
A cold northern lakeshore settlement of fishing cabins, boathouses and stilt-lodges built where forest meets water: fat debarked honey-pine logs, silvered weathered-cedar boards, rough multicolor glacial fieldstone, and greyed timber pilings standing in the shallows — every surface damp, algae-stained at the waterline and softened by lake mist. The architectural identity is WET LAKESIDE TIMBER-AND-STONE: each wall reads first as one solid load-bearing surface, its value coming from dark recessed chinking, deep mortar joints, algae-green waterline shadow and a heavy stone footing, never from a bright body. Palette: warm honey and amber debarked logs, silvery reddish-brown weathered cedar, grey-blue-rust glacial stone in dark mortar, greyed driftwood-timber pilings, with damp green algae and lichen flecks along every footing.
