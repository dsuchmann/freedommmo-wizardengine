## Aesthetic
A waterlogged bog-village of stilt shacks salvaged from whatever the marsh gives up: warped grey-brown sawn planks, stacked reddish mangrove logs chinked with bog-mud, wattle smeared with olive reed-daub, and heavy moss-furred post-and-beam frames infilled with woven cane and bark. The architectural identity is DAMP SALVAGED TIMBER — every wall reads first as one solid load-bearing waterlogged surface, its value coming from dark waterline staining, tarred sill courses and deep wet shadow, never from any bright body. Pervasive moss-green, algae bloom and a wet glistening sheen tie it together. Palette: damp grey-brown driftwood, reddish-brown mangrove bark, bog-olive mud and reed, dark tarred timber, deep moss-green and lichen, slick wet highlights — richly saturated, nothing pale or chalky.
