## Aesthetic
A treeless polar settlement built from frozen MASS — quarried ice blocks, carved snow blocks, driftwood lashed under taut seal-hide, and rimed frozen logs hung with icicles. The architectural identity is SOLID COLD MASONRY: every wall reads first as one continuous load-bearing block wall, its value driven by DARK frosted seams, deep blue facet-shadows and a banked stone-and-ice footing — NEVER from a bright body. Critically, no ice/snow/whitewashed surface is rendered near-white (PixelLab would alpha-matte it into mud): ice and snow are SOLID mid blue-white / cool periwinkle-grey blocks with crisp dark facet edges and visible shadow seams, sparkles kept as small specular flecks on a mid body. The four walls span a real tonal break — luminous cyan ice, matte cool-white snow, warm grey-tan hide, and dark frost-rimed timber. Palette: mid cyan-blue and slate blue-white ice, cool periwinkle-grey snow with blue seams, silver driftwood and oiled grey-tan hide, dark frost-rimed blue-grey timber with sparkling white rime, banked snow-grey stone footing.
