## Aesthetic
A dramatic settlement carved from cooled lava-stone and lit from within by restrained ember-glow tracing the seams — the hot counterpart to the mystic biome's cool moonlight. The architecture is heavy, glossy and dark-saturated (glossy black basalt, glassy charcoal obsidian, molten-crimson glazed brick) with ONE deliberately lighter member, ashcrete, held at a MID dove-grey so it never alpha-mattes away. Molten orange lives only in joints, mortar and footing-lines as a thin disciplined glow, never as neon or as see-through holes.
