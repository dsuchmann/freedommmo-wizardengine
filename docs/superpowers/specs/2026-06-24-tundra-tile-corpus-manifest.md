## Aesthetic
A treeless far-north settlement built from what the frozen ground gives up: stacked living turf, dark cut peat, drystone fieldstone, and whalebone framed in caribou hide. The architectural identity is EARTH-AND-BONE SHELTER — every wall reads first as one solid, low, thick, heat-hoarding load-bearing mass, its value coming from dark peaty joints, deep mortar shadow and damp earth, never from a bright body. The whalebone-and-hide build supplies the warm tonal break against the cool greens, browns and greys; its ivory bone is rendered as a SOLID mid cool-bone grey with dark facet edges, never near-white. Palette: mossy saturated turf green, deep chocolate-and-reddish peat brown, cool rust-flecked fieldstone grey, warm tan-and-ivory hide, with a unifying mossy grey-green stone footing and dark sinew-and-mortar shadow.
