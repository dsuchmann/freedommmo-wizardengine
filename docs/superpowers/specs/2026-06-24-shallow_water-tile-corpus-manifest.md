## Aesthetic
A tidal-shallows village of stilt huts and dock shacks built from whatever the warm coast gives up: sun-silvered driftwood patchwork, woven straw-gold reed, neatly milled weathered boardwalk planks, and shell-flecked lime daub, all standing over bright pale water on tarred posts. The architectural identity is SALT-WEATHERED COASTAL TIMBER AND REED — every wall reads first as one solid, load-bearing, spray-bleached surface whose value comes from dark plank gaps, deep weave shadow, tarred footings and rusty nail rows, never from a bright body. The daub stays a MID shell-grey, never a near-white that would dissolve. Palette: sun-silvered driftwood grey, warm honey salvage-brown, straw-gold reed, grey-tan milled plank, creamy shell-grey lime, dark tar plinths, rusty nail flecks and pink-white crushed shell sparkle.
