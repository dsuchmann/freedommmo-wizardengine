## Aesthetic
A hot, dry sub-Saharan grassland village built from sun-baked earth and woven plant matter: smooth hand-troweled ochre adobe, neat courses of tan-and-amber mud brick, basketwork walls of bent acacia thornwood packed with pale daub, and lightweight panels of golden woven savanna grass — all roofed in bundled grass, palm fronds, laid thorn-poles, or burnt-orange clay barrel tiles. The architectural identity is WARM SUN-DRIED EARTH AND WOVEN PLANT — every wall reads first as one solid load-bearing earthen or woven surface, its value coming from dark mortar joints, recessed daub shadow, deep terracotta footing and burnt-umber thornwood, NEVER from a bright body. Lightest safe value is mid golden-straw, never pale or white. Palette: warm ochre and golden-amber, tan mudbrick with dark-earth mortar, sun-bleached bone-tan thornwood, honey straw weave, deep terracotta plinths and burnt-orange tile, all under hot directional savanna sun.
