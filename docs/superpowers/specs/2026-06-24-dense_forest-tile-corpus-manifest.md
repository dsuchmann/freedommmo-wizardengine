## Aesthetic
A sunless old-growth woodland hamlet half-swallowed by the canopy: dwellings of fat moss-cloaked stacked logs, brooding tar-black timber, rammed root-laced forest earth, and deeply furrowed bark planks — every surface damp, shadowed, and ancient. The architectural identity is DARK DAMP TIMBER-AND-EARTH STRUCTURE: each wall reads first as one solid load-bearing surface, its value coming from black chinking shadow, deep grooves and wet boulder footing, never from a bright body. The four walls hold a real tonal break — mid moss-green, near-black tar-log, mid warm umber earth, and charcoal bark — while mixing stacked, woven and cut structures. Palette: emerald and viridian moss, tar-black and charcoal timber, deep brown furrows, rich umber earth, twisted roots and toadstool-red flecks, lichen grey-green, cold blue rim-light, and black wet boulders.
