## Aesthetic
A steaming equatorial rainforest village raised on stilts and lashed together from living jungle: golden-green bamboo poles bound with dark rattan, breathable basket-woven palm matting, lustrous red-brown tropical hardwood, and pale teak stilt-decks over pierced lattice — all under steep leaf and bamboo roofs that shed torrential rain. The architectural identity is WOVEN-AND-LASHED ORGANIC STRUCTURE: every wall reads first as one solid continuous load-bearing surface, its value pulled from dark rattan bindings, deep node-ring shadows, woven shadow-valleys and a damp hardwood footing — NEVER from a bright body. Palette: jade-and-straw bamboo, green-gold and tan woven palm, rich mahogany red-brown hardwood with golden grain, pale honey-teak boards, dark rattan and chocolate lashing, warm coral-terracotta and emerald roof tones, all richly saturated and humid.
