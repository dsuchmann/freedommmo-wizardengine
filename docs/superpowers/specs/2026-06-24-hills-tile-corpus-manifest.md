## Aesthetic
A stony upland pastoral village raised straight from the local hill geology: mortarless drystone, dressed quarried ashlar, lime-rendered rubble cottages and slate fish-scale cladding, all roofed in the same riven blue-grey slate and heavy stone flag. The architectural identity is COURSED HILLSTONE MASONRY warmed by lime render — every wall reads first as one solid load-bearing stone surface whose value comes from deep dark shadowed joints, chinking gaps and dark slate trim, NEVER from a bright body. Palette: cool grey-blue hillstone, warm buff banded sandstone, rust-tan lichen, dark steel-blue slate with faint purple-green iridescence, and a restrained mid lime-grey render (a tinted off-white, never stark white). A believable rough-to-woven spread: rough mortarless drystone, crisply dressed cut ashlar, smooth lime-rendered cottage, woven slate fish-scale hanging.
