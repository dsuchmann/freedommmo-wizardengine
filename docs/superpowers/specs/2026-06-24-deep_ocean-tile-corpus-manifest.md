## Aesthetic
A far-water deep-sea settlement built on barnacle-crusted stilt platforms and armored lighthouse towers standing in open ocean: salt-blasted driftwood pilings, riveted teal-grey iron hull plate, tar-caulked clinker ship-planking, and pale whalebone-ribbed huts, all glossed with pitch and rimed with white salt. The architectural identity is SALVAGED MARINE STRUCTURE — every wall reads first as one solid, opaque, load-bearing weatherskin that has survived constant spray, its value coming from glossy black pitch caulking, dark barnacle shadow, deep rivet seams and tar-soaked footings, NEVER from a bright body. The lightest tones (whalebone, salt rime) are held as a SOLID cool grey-white with dark seams and shadow, never near-white. Palette: weathered grey-brown driftwood, oiled umber and charcoal plank, deep teal-grey iron with orange rust and verdigris, white-ochre barnacle crust, bone-grey-white, glossy black pitch, brass nailheads.
