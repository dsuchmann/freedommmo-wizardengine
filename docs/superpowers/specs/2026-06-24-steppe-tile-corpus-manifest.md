## Aesthetic
A windswept treeless-plain settlement built from the ground it stands on — monolithic rammed earth, sun-dried adobe brick, cut prairie sod, and nomad felt lashed over willow lattice, all scoured warm and dusty by constant steppe wind. The architectural identity is EARTHEN MASS AND LASHED FELT: every wall reads first as one solid, thermally heavy, load-bearing earthen surface (or a taut solid felt skin), its value coming from dark formwork lines, deep mortar joints, root-shadowed seams and a dark stone footing — NEVER from a bright pale body. Construction runs from smooth monolithic earth, through coursed mud brick, to woven-look felt and shaggy cut sod. Palette: dusty tan + ochre + warm rose earth, baked adobe, root-dark brown-green sod with dried golden-straw fringe, cream-and-rust wool felt, weathered grey timber and woven cord, with grey fieldstone footings.
