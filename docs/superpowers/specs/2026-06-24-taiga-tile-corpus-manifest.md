## Aesthetic
A boreal pine-forest hamlet built entirely of conifer wood against deep cold: fat horizontally-stacked debarked pine logs, warm reddish board-and-batten planking, glossy black-brown pitch-tarred stave timber, and amber half-timber frames packed with pale ochre clay daub — all footed on dark river-cobble and capped with carved snow-shed eaves. The architectural identity is WARM SATURATED TIMBER STRUCTURE WITH A TAR-DARK COUNTERPOINT: every wall reads first as one solid continuous load-bearing wooden surface, its value coming from dark saddle-notch joints, deep moss chinking shadow, blackened tar and grey cobble footing — never from a bright body. Palette: honey-amber and golden pine, warm reddish sap-streaked planks, glossy near-black pine pitch with warm specular sheen, pale ochre daub, mossy green chinking, and dark grey river-cobble. The believable spread runs warm-round (logs) → warm-sawn (plank) → DARK tarred (pitch) → warm-framed pale-infill (log-and-daub), with the real tonal break at the glossy black pitch wall.
