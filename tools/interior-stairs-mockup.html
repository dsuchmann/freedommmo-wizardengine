Stairs & lift — clearer up/down flow

Today one yellow tile = stairs (up AND down) and a blue tile = lift (up AND down) — no direction, and chaining floors is fiddly. Proposed: a landing you arrive on, a gap, then a dedicated ▲ UP tile and ▼ DOWN tile so direction is obvious and you climb deliberately. The lift stays one tile but reads as a lift (pick a floor).
