Building shadows — live

Drives the real drawBuildingShadows() + the real DayNightCycle.sun(). Façades are drawn with the same facadeRect() the drape lands on, so what you see is the shipped geometry.

What you're looking at: a tall 5-storey tower (centre) ringed by shorter houses + grass tufts.

• Ground shadow shoots away from the sun — straight down at noon, long to the west at dawn, long to the east at dusk; length grows with storeys + low sun.

• Drape: where the tower's shadow reaches a neighbour it climbs that wall — but only as high as the caster is tall (a short building can't scale a tall one).

• Grass respects height: scrub to dusk (long shadows) and toggle the checkbox — grass shadows that would streak onto the tower roof are cut off at the building (they sample the height mask; grass = 0 storeys < any building). "show blocked" draws the cut shadows in red; "show height mask" overlays the mask the grass samples.

• Everything fades out at night.
