Building shadows — live

Drives the real drawBuildingShadows() + the real DayNightCycle.sun(). Façades are drawn with the same facadeRect() the drape lands on, so what you see is the shipped geometry.

What you're looking at: a tall 5-storey tower (centre) ringed by shorter houses.

• Ground shadow shoots away from the sun — straight down at noon, long to the west at dawn, long to the east at dusk; length grows with storeys + low sun.

• Drape: where the tower's shadow reaches a neighbour, it climbs that neighbour's south wall instead of lying flat — the "drench a surface at a different height" bit.

• Both fade out at night.
